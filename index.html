Glimmerdeep Privacy Policy

Liquidpixel Studio · Last updated 27 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how Glimmerdeep (the "Game"), an Android game published on Google Play by Liquidpixel Studio ("we", "us"), handles your information.

Summary

Glimmerdeep does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. The Game works fully offline and does not connect to the internet.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The Game does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details, and it has no accounts or sign-in.

Data stored on your device

The Game saves your progress (such as stars, unlocked dives, guides, settings and your in-game Photo log) locally on your device so you can carry on where you left off. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the Game, clearing its data or using the in-game "Reset progress" option deletes it.

Permissions

The Game only uses the vibration permission, to give haptic feedback during play. You can turn vibration off in the Game's settings. The Game does not request internet access.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

Glimmerdeep contains no ads, no analytics or tracking tools and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The in-game "Photo log" only records which sea creatures you have spotted in the Game. It does not use your camera.

Purchases

Glimmerdeep is a paid game bought through Google Play. Payments are handled entirely by Google, and we do not receive your payment details. See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google handles purchase information.

Children

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, email liquidpixelstudio1987@gmail.com.
